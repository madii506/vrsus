vrsus docs

8 min read · copy link
the uncomfortable part first. vrsus is custodial. The engine holds each duel’s wallet, the wallet pump.fun pays both coins’ creator fees to, and it pays the pot out when the event settles. That is the only way a pot can pay itself out without someone signing. It is the same model wrld and rulr use. Memecoins can go to zero, and nothing here is advice.

01overview

Every launch on vrsus is a duel: one real event and two pump.fun coins, a YES coin and a NO coin. You pick a side by buying a coin. Both coins are created by the same wallet, so pump.fun pays both coins’ creator fees to one place: the pot. When the event’s source gives a final answer, the engine pays the pot in SOL to everyone holding the winning coin, pro rata to the size of their bag. After that, fees keep coming in as long as the coins trade, and every 24 hours they go to the winning side again.

02pairing

  • Pick a real event from one of the three sources. The server re-reads the source before it accepts it: the market must be open, the game not started, the price minute at least an hour away.
  • Name the two coins and give each a picture (or draw the green and red coins), and choose a first buy for each side.
  • vrsus records the duel, derives its wallet and both mint addresses, and tells you one amount to send.
  • You send that one SOL transfer. The engine creates both coins from the duel’s wallet with your first buys, then sends you the tokens of both and any unused SOL.
  • The coins’ pump.fun descriptions carry the question, the exact rule and the duel’s fingerprint.
two first buysyour choice, 0.01–2 SOL each (+2% for fees)
pump.fun rent and fees, two coinsabout 0.06 SOL, the unused part comes back

A duel never settles on a headline, a post or a guess. If the source doesn’t answer, the duel waits and its page says so.

04how fast

The engine runs every minute (Vercel Cron, with a GitHub Actions backup and every page visit nudging it). Each pass reads every live duel’s source. The first pass that sees a final answer settles the duel in the same pass: final claim, snapshot, payouts.

  • sports: within about a minute of ESPN marking the game final.
  • price: within about a minute of the deadline, once Coinbase publishes that minute’s candle.
  • world: on the first pass after Polymarket resolves the market, which follows Polymarket’s own resolution process.

Holders are read at the moment of settlement. Trading doesn’t stop before that: if you buy the winning side after the news but before the source settles, you hold it at the snapshot like everyone else.

05payouts

  • The engine lists every token account of the winning coin straight from the chain and adds them up per wallet. Pools, the bonding curve and the duel’s own wallet are left out. If the RPC won’t list every account, the round says so and uses the 20 largest.
  • The pot is the duel wallet’s balance minus the gas reserve and a little for the payout transactions.
  • Each wallet gets floor(pot × its bag ÷ all bags) lamports. Payouts under 0.0001 SOL are skipped and stay in the pot for the next round.
  • The snapshot (every wallet and its bag) is published with the round, with its SHA-256 hash over side|pot|owner:amount,owner:amount,… in the published order, and a Merkle root over what each wallet is owed (see verify).
  • Payouts go out in transactions of 12. Each payout is marked with its transaction before it is sent and checked on the next pass, so nothing is paid twice and nothing is skipped.

06verify a payout

Every round publishes two commitments before a single lamport moves: the snapshot hash (SHA-256 over the whole holder list) and a Merkle root over what each wallet is owed. Three ways to check them, from most convenient to trusting vrsus the least:

  • In the browser. On a duel page, verify this round recomputes the hash, the root and every split from the published snapshot; check on chain reads every payout transaction back from a public RPC and matches each transfer. On the home page, check your wallet walks your leaf up to the published root.
  • In the terminal. verify <#> [round], proof <#> <round> <wallet> and chain <#> [round] do the same, as text.
  • Without us. Download a duel’s proof bundle and run verify.mjs (below). It re-reads the source itself and reads the chain from any RPC you choose.

the merkle tree

# one leaf per wallet, in the snapshot's published order leaf = sha256("L|" + round + "|" + side + "|" + owner + "|" + bag + "|" + lamports) node = sha256("N|" + left + "|" + right) # hex strings; an odd node at the end of a level moves up unchanged lamports = floor(pot × bag ÷ all bags) # the leaf commits to what is owed; payouts under 100,000 lamports are skipped

verify.mjs

One file, no dependencies, Node 18 or newer. Point it at a duel and, optionally, at your own RPC:

$curl -sO https://vrsus-seven.vercel.app/verify.mjs && node verify.mjs <duel-id> --rpc https://your-rpc
  1. the event: re-reads Polymarket, ESPN or Coinbase directly and applies the published rule.
  2. the pot: reads both coins’ pump.fun bonding curves and checks their creator is the duel wallet, so both coins’ fees really fill one pot.
  3. the rounds: recomputes the bag total, the snapshot hash, the Merkle root and every split.
  4. the chain: reads every payout transaction and matches each transfer from the duel wallet, wallet by wallet, lamport by lamport.

It exits with code 0 when everything checks out and 1 when anything doesn’t, so it can run in CI. Pass --bundle file.json to check a bundle you saved earlier.

07void

If the source voids the event (a cancelled game, a voided market), the pot is split in half: one round pays the YES holders, one round pays the NO holders, each pro rata.

08custody

Each duel’s wallet and both mint keys are derived on the server from one master secret and the duel id, using HMAC-SHA256. They are never stored. The wallet is the pump.fun creator of both coins.

  • The engine’s code moves SOL in four ways only: the launch hand-back, refunds, vrsus’s 5%, and payouts to the snapshot. There is no sell and no withdraw.
  • What you are trusting: that the engine keeps running, and that the master secret stays secret. If either fails, payouts stop; the SOL stays in the duel’s wallet.

09fees

When at least 0.003 SOL of creator fees is waiting, the engine claims it into the duel’s wallet. vrsus takes 5% of each claim. Trades go through PumpPortal’s local transaction API, which adds its own 0.5%.
