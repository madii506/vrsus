WHERE THE MONEY GOES

one creator wallet, two coins, one pot

      
Both coins in a duel are created by the same wallet, so pump.fun pays both coins’ creator fees to one place: the pot. Trading on either side fills it. When the source says how the event ended, the engine snapshots every holder of the winning coin, publishes the snapshot and its hash, and pays the pot out pro rata to the size of each bag. After that, every 24 hours, new fees go to the winning side again.

FAQ

    

      
What is vrsus?
A pump.fun launchpad where every launch is a duel: one real event, a YES coin and a NO coin. You pick a side by buying a coin. Both coins’ creator fees go to one pot, and when the event’s source says how it ended, the pot is paid in SOL to the holders of the winning coin.

      
How fast does it settle?
The engine runs every minute. Sports duels settle within about a minute of ESPN marking the game final. Price duels settle within about a minute of their deadline. World duels settle on the first pass after Polymarket resolves the market, which follows Polymarket’s own resolution process.

      
How do I check a payout?
Every round publishes the snapshot of holders it paid from, its SHA-256 hash, a Merkle root over what each wallet is owed, and every payout with its transaction. Paste your wallet under CHECK YOUR WALLET to see your leaf and your path to the root, or run verify and chain in the terminal. To check without trusting this site at all, download a duel’s proof bundle and run verify.mjs against any RPC (see the docs).

      
Who counts as a holder?
Every wallet holding the winning coin at the moment the engine settles, read straight from the chain. Pools, the bonding curve and the duel’s own wallet are left out. If the RPC won’t list every holder, the round says so and pays the 20 largest.

      
What if the game is cancelled or the market is voided?
The duel is void and the pot is split in half: one half to the YES holders, one half to the NO holders, each pro rata.

      
Is it custodial?
Yes. Each duel’s wallet is derived and held by the engine, the same model as wrld and rulr. That is what lets it settle without anyone signing. If that isn’t for you, don’t pair here.

      
What does pairing cost?
Two first buys (one on each side), about 0.06 SOL of pump.fun rent and fees for the two coins, and a 0.015 SOL gas reserve that stays in the duel’s wallet. Anything left over goes back to you with your first-buy tokens.
