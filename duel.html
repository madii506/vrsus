VERIFY WITHOUT US
download the proof bundle

        

          
The bundle is this duel’s whole record in one JSON file: the event, the rule, the reading it settled on, every snapshot with its hash and Merkle root, every payout and its transaction. verify.mjs checks it against the world: it re-reads the source itself, recomputes every split, hash and root, reads every payout back from the RPC you choose, and checks that both coins’ pump.fun creator is the duel wallet. No dependencies, Node 18 or newer.
